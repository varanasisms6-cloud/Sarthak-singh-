💡 Why Street Kashi?

A local tourism discovery platform designed to connect travellers with authentic Kashi experiences and local service providers — while making trip planning simpler.

For TravellersDiscover, compare and enquire about stays, guides, food and experiences.
For Local BusinessesDigital visibility for hotels, guest houses, guides, boats, restaurants and shops.
For the EcosystemBuild a trusted local marketplace around responsible and verified tourism.
